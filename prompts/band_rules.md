# Age band rules

Inject the relevant block as `{band_rules}` in the generation user message. The judge also uses the age range below.

## early — ages 3–5
- Length: **write a FULL story of about 430 words — roughly 26–32 short sentences. It MUST be at least 360 words; a shorter story is rejected and regenerated, so keep going until the whole arc is told — do not wrap up after just a few lines.** Aim for the full ~430 (about 2.5–3 minutes read aloud): long enough to get absorbing, short enough to hold a young child's attention. Give each beat room to breathe — the hook, the small wonder/problem building in the middle, the warm resolution, the sleepy wind-down — rather than rushing to the ending. Don't pad past ~520.
- Sentences: short and simple. Concrete, familiar objects and feelings.
- Use soft repetition and gentle rhythm (repeated phrases, soothing sounds).
- Exactly one tiny problem, solved with kindness. No real danger at any point.
- Small cast: the child plus 1–2 characters.

## older — ages 6–8
- Length: **write a FULL story of about 450 words — roughly 28–36 sentences. It MUST be at least 360 words; a shorter story is rejected and regenerated, so develop the whole arc rather than wrapping up early.** (about 3–5 minutes read aloud). Give the arc room — setup, a mild challenge, rising effort, resolution, a calm close — without padding. Up to ~650 is fine.
- Sentences: a little longer; richer but still familiar vocabulary.
- A slightly bigger arc with one mild, never-scary challenge.
- Small cast: the child plus 2–3 characters.

## Always — bedtime overlay (applies to BOTH bands)
- The arc de-escalates toward calm. The final ~20% is soft, slow, and sleepy.
- End safe, warm, and settled. No cliffhangers, no final burst of excitement.
